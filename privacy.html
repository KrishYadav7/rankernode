Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · applies to rankernode.example and the RankerNode apps for Android and Windows

RankerNode ("RankerNode", "we") runs an online learning platform for students preparing for JEE, NEET and Class 11–12 exams. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information and we do not show advertising.

1. Information we collect

WhatExamplesWhy
Account detailsName, username, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a secure hash), optional profile photoTo create and secure your account, sign you in and contact you about it
Learning activityCourses opened, video and reading progress, quiz and exam answers and scores, streaks, certificatesTo show your progress, grade practice papers and issue certificates
Content you createHandwritten notes and notebooks, highlights, reviews, contributions, help-desk messages, files you upload for subjective answers or doubtsTo store and show them to you (and, for reviews and approved contributions, to other students)
AI Doubt SolverQuestions you type and images or files you attachTo generate an answer (see "Service providers")
PurchasesCourses, materials or plans you bought, amount, date and payment referenceTo unlock what you paid for and provide receipts. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay and never reach our servers.
Device and security dataIP address, browser or app version, device type, sign-in times, signed-in devicesTo keep accounts secure, limit sharing of accounts, prevent abuse and fix problems
Usage statisticsPages visited and time spent, counted per dayTo understand which materials help students and to improve the platform

The apps can use your camera or files only when you choose to attach a photo or file. Screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the apps to protect course content.

2. How we use information

  • to provide the platform: accounts, courses, quizzes, notes, certificates and the help desk;
  • to process purchases and subscriptions;
  • to send account emails such as sign-in codes, password resets and security alerts;
  • to protect the platform and its content against misuse and account sharing;
  • to improve courses and features using aggregated usage statistics.

3. Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run RankerNode, and only what they need:

  • Razorpay — payments and subscriptions;
  • Google (Gemini) and Groq — generating AI Doubt Solver answers from the question and attachments you send;
  • Cloudinary — storing uploaded images and files;
  • Email delivery services — sending sign-in codes and account emails;
  • Hosting and database providers — running our servers and storing data securely.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to administrative tools is limited and protected by additional verification.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account data and the content you create: until you delete your account.
  • Security and access logs: deleted automatically after 12 months.
  • Daily usage statistics: deleted automatically after 6 months.
  • Payment records held by Razorpay: kept by Razorpay as the law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction — see and edit your details in your profile, or ask us.
  • Delete your account — in the website or app open Settings › Account › Delete my account, or use rankernode.example/delete-account. Your account and its data are deleted immediately.
  • Emails — account and security emails are part of the service; you can ask us to stop any other emails.

7. Children

RankerNode is meant for college and university students. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you on the platform.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the Help Desk or contact options on rankernode.example.
