Delete your RankerNode account

You can delete your RankerNode student account at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • your profile: name, username, email, phone number, photo and password;
  • your learning progress, quiz and exam results, streaks and certificates;
  • your handwritten notes and notebooks, help-desk messages, reviews and contributions;
  • access to courses and plans you bought — they cannot be restored to a new account.

What is kept

  • security and access logs, deleted automatically within 12 months;
  • daily usage counts, deleted automatically within 6 months;
  • payment records held by Razorpay, as the law requires.

See the full privacy policy.

Option 1 — inside the website or app

  1. Sign in to RankerNode.
  2. Open your profile menu (top right) → Settings.
  3. Under Account, choose Delete my account and confirm with your password.

Option 2 — right here

Forgot your password? Reset it from the sign-in page first. Admin and professor accounts are removed by the RankerNode team on request.
